# Hi, I'm Umar 👋

I'm a **combined MS–PhD student and BK21 fellow** in the [Ulsan Industrial AI (UIAI) Lab](https://uiai.ulsan.ac.kr/), **University of Ulsan, South Korea**, supervised by [Prof. Jong-Myon Kim](https://uiai.ulsan.ac.kr/professor/).

I work on **fault diagnosis and remaining useful life (RUL) prediction** for industrial machines such as milling machines, bearings, centrifugal pumps and pipelines. Most of my work uses **acoustic emission and vibration signals**, combining time-frequency analysis with deep learning, with a focus on models that are explainable and report their own uncertainty.

GitHub is where I keep code, implementations and experiments that go with my research.

## Research interests

- Condition-based monitoring and fault diagnosis
- Remaining useful life prediction and degradation modelling
- Time-frequency analysis of acoustic emission and vibration signals
- Uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction
- Explainable AI for industrial systems (Grad-CAM, SHAP)
- Domain adaptation across machines and operating conditions
- Physics-guided, self-supervised and few-shot learning
